Sol Index: logo options, round 2

Six directions in six different styles. Colour is currentColor plus one Mars rust (#c9501f). Light and dark panels force their background. Each row: the mark at 16, 32, 64, 256 px, the simplified favicon version (fav 16, 32), then the lockup by height. Unofficial project: no NASA or JPL marks. Round 1 is in the parent folder.

Option 5. Line art

line-art helmet with Mars in the visor

When it fits: Docs, README header, outlined or stamped uses, one-colour print. Profile helmet; the visor opens onto the front edge so it does not read as an eye.

Favicon (16 px): collar removed, stroke 3 to 4.5, planet 5.5 to 6. Shown as "fav" next to the full mark.

Option 6. Flat emblem

round badge with the Martian limb, a moon and stars

When it fits: Hero, stickers, social card, merch: a patch-like badge with the Martian limb under a night sky. The negative space is a real cut-out (mask), so it works on any background.

Favicon (16 px): ring and stars removed, only the dome and the moon cut-out stay. Shown as "fav" next to the full mark.

Option 7. Lettermark

monogram S built from two orbit arcs, Mars as the end dot

When it fits: Typographic identity: app icon, avatar, favicon where the name matters more than the planet. Reads as a plain S first, Mars is the quiet detail.
