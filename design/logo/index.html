Sol Index: logo options

Four directions, each as a mark and a mark plus wordmark. Colour is currentColor plus one Mars rust (#c9501f); both panels below force a light or dark background so the pair can be judged in one glance. Unofficial project: no NASA or JPL marks. Numbers under each sample are pixel sizes (the mark at 16, 32, 64, 256; the lockup by height).

Option 1. Helmet with Mars in the visor

When it fits: Hero spots: landing page header, about page, stickers, social card. Most characterful, but too detailed below ~32 px.
