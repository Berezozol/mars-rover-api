Range of sols

The track shows every sol; the shaded hatched part on the right is not published yet. Drag a handle, drag the highlighted span to move it, or focus a handle: arrow keys move it by 1 sol, PageUp and PageDown by 10, Home and End to the limit. The default range ends at the newest published sol, not at the rover’s latest sol.
