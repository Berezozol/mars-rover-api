№ is the sample’s order on the NASA page, not the tube serial number. Names and places are shown as published (including aliases). The sealing date has no time zone on the source page; the sol is the reliable key. Height is the core length in centimetres; “TBD” means the page has no date or sol yet. “Photos of the tube” = photos of that sol from the CACHECAM camera; the link is by sol only, so the tube may be out of frame. Dossier = the science report (PDF) on the Planetary Data System.
