The Mars helicopter flew 72 times between sol 58 and sol 1035 (2021-04-19 to 2024-01-18, Pacific time). Sols are Perseverance sols. Open a flight’s photos to see everything taken that sol.

Click a column heading to sort; click again to reverse.
Ingenuity flights

Distance is horizontal, in metres per flight. A dash after the arrow means the flight log does not name a landing site (usually the same site as take-off). Highlighted rows hold a record (highest, longest, fastest). Dates are Pacific time as in the flight log; the sol is the reliable key. Photos counts all Ingenuity photos of that sol, not only those from the flight.
