What we store

For a key we store your email address, the dates it was created and confirmed, your keys (only as hashes) and the number of requests per day. The address is used to issue the key and to contact you about abuse; we send nothing but the messages you asked for and never share it with anyone. Email is sent through Google (Gmail). Unconfirmed requests are deleted within a day, request counters after 90 days, an account after 24 months without a request. You can delete your data at any time: in My keys with your key, via the link on Delete my data, or by replying to any of our emails.
