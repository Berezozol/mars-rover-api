Mars Rover API

An open, unofficial replacement for the archived Mars Rover Photos API.

This is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by NASA or JPL. Photos and metadata come from the public raw image feeds and archives published by NASA/JPL.

Migrating

Moving from the old Mars Rover Photos API takes three steps:

  1. Change the host and keep the rest of the URL. 
    https://api.nasa.gov/mars-photos/...  →  $origin/mars-photos/...
  2. No key needed. An api_key parameter is accepted and ignored, so existing clients keep working unchanged.
  3. Both paths work: $origin/api/v1/... and $origin/mars-photos/api/v1/... serve the same API.

Examples

v1, compatible with the old API:

curl "$origin/mars-photos/api/v1/rovers/curiosity/photos?sol=1000&camera=fhaz"
curl "$origin/mars-photos/api/v1/rovers/perseverance/latest_photos"
curl "$origin/mars-photos/api/v1/manifests/opportunity"

v2, the new API:

curl "$origin/api/v2/rovers"
curl "$origin/api/v2/rovers/perseverance/photos?sol=100&per_page=50"
curl "$origin/api/v2/photos?rovers=curiosity,perseverance&date_min=2026-11-06&sort=-taken_at&per_page=50"
curl "$origin/api/v2/photos/stats?rovers=perseverance&group_by=month"
curl "$origin/api/v2/photos/batch?ids=81234,81235,900000000"

Stats counts photos and sols with the first and last date, grouped by rover, camera, year or month (group_by). sample_type accepts only full; date_min/date_max filter by the date of the sol, not of each photo. Batch takes 1 to 100 comma-separated ids; ids that are not found are returned in missing instead of a 404.

GET /api/v2/photos searches several rovers at once: rovers (comma-separated slugs; default all), sol/sol_min/sol_max or earth_date/date_min/date_max (not both), camera (up to 10 codes), sort (-sol, sol, -taken_at, taken_at), local_time_min/local_time_max or golden_hour=true, min_width/min_height, and near=LAT,LON with radius_km (one rover only: curiosity or perseverance). page × per_page must not exceed 10 000. Heavy searches (for example date, local-time or size filters without sol) may answer 503 service_busy with a Retry-After header; retry later or narrow the query.

Every v2 photo has two time fields. taken_at is Earth time (UTC). local_time ({"time": "17:40:12", "kind": "lmst", "planet": "mars"}) is the local solar time on Mars where the rover stood: lmst (mean time) for Curiosity, Perseverance and Ingenuity, ltst (true time) for Spirit and Opportunity. It is null when the source gives no time. With near, each item also has near_stop: the rover stop the photo is tied to (sol, site, drive, lat, lon) and distance_km to your point.

Photo lists (/api/v2/photos, /api/v2/rovers/{rover}/photos, .../photos/latest, /api/v2/photos/batch) accept fields, a comma-separated list of photo keys in lower case (for example fields=sol,img_src,camera). id and credit are always included. It only shrinks the response: it does not speed up searches or change rate limits. An unknown key gives 422. Each photo also has mast: the camera direction as azimuth (0 to 360) and elevation (-90 to 90) in degrees. The angles are relative to the rover body, not to north, so they are not a compass bearing. It is null when the source gives no angles.

Interactive documentation of every endpoint: /docs. Machine-readable: /openapi.json, and a short reference for LLM assistants: /llms.txt.
